## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Before you start using Library DApp, make sure you have:

- ✅ **MetaMask Extension**: Installed and configured ([Setup Guide](MetaMask-Setup.md))
- ✅ **Ethereum Account**: With some ETH for gas fees
- ✅ **Web Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, or any modern browser

### First Time Access

1. **Visit the Application**: Navigate to the Library DApp URL
2. **Connect Wallet**: Click "Connect Wallet" and select MetaMask
3. **Accept Connection**: Approve the connection request in MetaMask
4. **Register Account**: Complete the registration process

## 👤 User Registration

### Step-by-Step Registration

1. **Connect MetaMask**
   - Click the "Connect Wallet" button
   - Select MetaMask from the wallet options
   - Approve the connection request

2. **Choose Your Role**
   - **Student**: Access to standard borrowing features
   - **Professor**: Extended borrowing privileges and longer due dates

3. **Complete Registration**
   - Enter your full name
   - Confirm your role selection
   - Submit the registration transaction
   - Wait for blockchain confirmation

### Registration Requirements

- Valid Ethereum address with MetaMask
- Small amount of ETH for gas fees
- Unique wallet address (one account per wallet)

## 📖 Browsing Books

### Catalog Features

The book catalog offers multiple ways to find what you're looking for:

#### Search and Filter Options
- **Search by Title**: Use the search bar to find specific books
- **Filter by Author**: Browse books by your favorite authors
- **Category Filter**: Explore books by subject or genre
- **Availability Filter**: Show only available books

#### Book Information
Each book displays:
- 📚 **Title and Author**
- 🖼️ **Cover Image** (stored on IPFS)
- 📊 **Availability Status**
- 📅 **Due Date** (if borrowed)
- ⭐ **Book Rating** (if available)

### Viewing Book Details

Click on any book to see:
- Complete description
- Publication details
- Current availability
- Borrowing history
- IPFS links for additional resources

## 📥 Borrowing Books

### How to Borrow

1. **Find Available Book**
   - Browse the catalog
   - Ensure the book shows "Available" status

2. **Initiate Borrowing**
   - Click the "Borrow" button
   - Review the borrowing terms
   - Confirm the transaction in MetaMask

3. **Transaction Confirmation**
   - Wait for blockchain confirmation
   - Receive confirmation notification
   - Book appears in your dashboard

### Borrowing Limits

| User Role | Max Books | Loan Period |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Student | 3 books | 7 days |
| Professor | 5 books | 14 days |

### Important Notes

- ⚠️ **Gas Fees**: Each borrowing transaction requires ETH for gas
- 🕒 **Due Dates**: Return books on time to maintain good reputation
- 📱 **Notifications**: Check your dashboard for due date reminders

## 📤 Returning Books

### Return Process

1. **Access Your Dashboard**
   - Go to the "My Books" section
   - View your current borrowed books

2. **Initiate Return**
   - Click "Return" next to the book
   - Confirm the return transaction
   - Pay gas fees for the transaction

3. **Confirmation**
   - Wait for blockchain confirmation
   - Book becomes available for others
   - Reputation score updated

### Return Status

- ✅ **On Time**: +1 reputation point
- ⚠️ **Late**: -2 reputation points
- 🚫 **Very Late** (>3 days): -5 reputation points

## ⭐ Reputation System

### How Reputation Works

Your reputation score (0-100) affects:
- **Borrowing Privileges**: Higher scores allow more books
- **Loan Extensions**: Good reputation enables extensions
- **Priority Access**: Higher reputation gets priority on popular books

### Reputation Levels

| Score Range | Level | Benefits |
|-------------|-------|----------|
| 90-100 | Premium | Extended loans, priority booking |
| 75-89 | Gold | Multiple books, longer periods |
| 50-74 | Silver | Standard privileges |
| 0-49 | Bronze | Limited borrowing |

### Improving Your Reputation

- ✅ **Return books on time**
- ✅ **Keep books in good condition**
- ✅ **Follow library rules**
- ✅ **Participate in community activities**

## 💡 Tips and Best Practices

### Maximize Your Experience

1. **Set Reminders**: Use calendar apps to track due dates
